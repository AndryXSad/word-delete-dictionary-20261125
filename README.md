One word to erase from the dictionary forever, with nothing else attached.
A judge model reads entries two at a time and picks the funnier deletion.

> impossible

The word was chosen by a local pairwise tournament over 22 candidates, scored
on the judge brief's criteria in order: a real word a reader recognises; funny
at first sight; the humour comes from the word itself; a fresh pick; exactly
one word, spelled correctly. Erase "impossible" and nothing is impossible any
more — the joke is born from the word's own meaning, lands instantly, and is
not a field cliche (moist / literally / synergy / whatever were all rejected
for freshness, and the visible field entries irregardless, guesstimate and
Tomorrow were avoided as duplicates).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
